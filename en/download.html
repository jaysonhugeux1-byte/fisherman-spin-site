Release notes and download details · 18+ only

Install and start

  1. If you already use Fisherman, export a backup from Imports and close the app.
  2. Run the downloaded installer from this official page and choose your installation language.
  3. Open Fisherman and select English in the app’s language selector.
  4. Create an account or sign in, then import one completed EUR session and compare the totals.

If Windows blocks the installer, check its source and the release details before deciding whether to run it.

Follow the first-session guide →

The first-launch guide

Choose Guide me on your first launch, or reopen Tutorials from the menu. Six short guides cover imports, ranges, strategy maps, statistics and training games. Reading progress is saved.

Some games need filled-in ranges or strategy maps. Complete strategy analysis needs both preflop ranges and postflop maps; ordinary session results appear after import.

After the trial

A new account includes 14 days of Complete access without a payment card. You can then keep the Free plan, limited to your latest 2,000 Spins, or choose a subscription from Account. Joining the pilot does not reset an existing trial.
