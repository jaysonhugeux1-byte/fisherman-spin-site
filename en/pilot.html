Recruiting five volunteers

Test the English experience with your real session.

We are looking for five English-speaking Spin players on Windows. The aim is to validate installation, a real EUR import and whether the review tools help enough to use again.

Tell us your room and regional client before sending files. We will confirm whether your format is suitable for the pilot. A pilot application is an email, not a purchase or a subscription.

Apply by email

No attachment is required. Your email app opens with room, currency and Windows questions. Send the application yourself when ready.

A short, practical pilot

  1. First session · about 20 minutes

    Install the English build, import a small completed EUR session, compare totals, set up one range or map and try one Find the Leak round. Report any blocker and one useful result.

  2. Day 3 · remove the first friction

    Reopen the app and review another session. Tell us about confusing terms, missed histories or a step you could not complete. The founder can reply by email to help diagnose the specific issue.

  3. Day 7 · would you return?

    Tell us whether you used it again, which screen you returned to, what is missing and whether it is worth continuing. An honest “I stopped using it” is useful feedback.

Feedback stays voluntary.

There is no pilot-specific background usage tracking. You choose what to report by email. The app still uses its normal account and subscription services, described in the privacy policy (French).

For an import problem, start with the error text, app version, room and currency. If a history sample is needed later, agree what to share and remove identifying details from a copy. Keep the original on your PC.
